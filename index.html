Summer Capital Must Visit
Srinagar, J&K

The Crown of Kashmir

Srinagar, the summer capital of Jammu & Kashmir, is home to the iconic Dal Lake with floating houseboats, shikara rides and vibrant markets. Surrounded by snow-capped peaks and Mughal gardens, it is Kashmir's cultural and geographical heart.

Best SeasonMarch – October
Altitude1,585 metres
From Airport~15 km · 20 mins
Stay OptionsHouseboat & Hotels

Gulmarg, J&K

Meadow of Flowers

Gulmarg is one of Asia's premier ski destinations, famous for the Gulmarg Gondola — one of the world's highest cable cars. In summer it transforms into a lush meadow with breathtaking Himalayan views.

Best SeasonDec–Feb & Apr–Oct
Altitude2,650 metres
From Srinagar~52 km · 1.5 hrs
Famous ForGondola, Skiing, Snow

Top Activities

Valley of Shepherds
Pahalgam, J&K

Valley of Shepherds

Pahalgam is a scenic hill resort known for lush meadows, Betaab Valley, Aru Valley and the Lidder River. The origin point of the Amarnath Yatra, offering excellent trout fishing and horse riding.

Best SeasonApril – November
Altitude2,130 metres
From Srinagar~95 km · 2.5 hrs
Famous ForBetaab Valley, Baisaran

Top Activities

Meadow of Gold
Sonamarg, J&K

Meadow of Gold

Sonamarg is the gateway to the Himalayas and base for the Thajiwas Glacier trek. Pristine alpine scenery with the Sindh River flowing through it — one of Kashmir's most spectacular destinations.

Best SeasonMay – October
Altitude2,740 metres
From Srinagar~80 km · 2.5 hrs
Famous ForGlacier Trek, Camping

Doodhpathri, J&K

Hidden Valley of Milk

Doodhpathri, meaning "Valley of Milk," is a pristine offbeat destination near Budgam with white milky streams, lush meadows and emerald forests. A raw, untouched Kashmir experience that few get to witness.

Best SeasonMay – September
Altitude2,730 metres
